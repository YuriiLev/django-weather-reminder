Pick a city, choose how often, and get the forecast delivered by email or webhook.

How it works

  1. Create an account with your email address.
  2. Subscribe to one or more cities.
  3. Choose a delivery interval — every 1, 3, 6 or 12 hours.
  4. Weather arrives automatically. Change or cancel any time.
